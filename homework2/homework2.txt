3. What algebraic structure (group, monoid, semigroup, etc) is all even integers under addition

closed : a=2k1, b=2k2 => a+b = 2k1+2k2 = 2(k1+k2)
associative : (a+b)+c=a+(b+c)
identity element : 0
inverse : every even integer x∈Z , -x∈Z

all even integers under addition are group.

4.What algebraic structure is all odd integers under multiplication?

closed : a=2k1+1, b=2k2+1 => a+b = (2k1+1)*(2k2+1) = 2k1(2k2+1)+(2k2+1)= 4k1k2+2k1+2k2+1
associative : (a*b)*c=a*(b*c)
identity element : 1
inverse : every even integer x∈Z , 1/x!∈Z
all odd integers under multiplication is a monoid.
